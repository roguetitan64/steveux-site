What 256 versions actually taught

16layouts, each fully built
16brand identities
256combinations, live-swappable
4that deserve to exist
iSpeed is real

Fifteen alternate portfolios — not mockups, working pages with their own interactions — took roughly a day of AI-paired iteration. The 0→1 cost of a direction has collapsed to nearly nothing. That part of the hype is true.

iiSo are the defects

256 combinations means 256 sets of contrast failures, broken hovers, and layouts wearing brands that fight them. Try Poster red on the Wiki, or CRT green on the Broadsheet — the matrix makes bad taste one click reproducible. No team reviews 256 of anything.

iiiVerdicts are the product

The lasting output is the written rationale: the One-Pager is the strongest challenger to the real site, the Ledger could ship tomorrow, the Map should be stolen as a toggle, the Terminal belongs behind an easter egg — and twelve directions were correctly killed on paper.

ivThe skill inverted

When generation is free, the craft moves upstream: choosing which directions deserve tokens before you burn them, and stopping when the answer is clear. This page is the argument — and the confession that it took building 256 versions to make it.
