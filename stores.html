Things people miss in store design.

The numbers I design against, with the source. Each one maps to a feature above.

57%Share of viewing time spent above the fold. If your best seller isn’t there, the fold is wasted.NN/g eyetracking
+270%Purchase likelihood with five reviews visible versus none; larger for higher-priced items.Spiegel Research Center, Northwestern
40%Cart abandonments where extra costs were the reason. The largest issue in all e-commerce experiences: users hate the feeling of hidden costs.Baymard, checkout survey
