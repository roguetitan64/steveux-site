One portfolio, 256 ways

Same content, sixteen layouts, sixteen brands. Here are three of them. Terminal Ledger One-pager all fifteen →
What it is Sixteen alternate layouts, each with its own brand. A terminal, a broadsheet, a pinboard, end credits. Then a skin matrix so every brand can dress every layout. 16 × 16 = 256 combinations of the same content. Why it exists To find out how fast an AI pair goes 0→1→256, and where the speed stops helping. Because 256 combinations also means 256 sets of website issues. The actual skill is picking the direction before you burn the tokens, and knowing when to stop.
